# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** September 14, 2026

## Introduction
This Privacy Policy outlines how the "Reminder" (Business & Ledger Management) app collects, uses, and protects your information.

## Data Collection & Storage
**We do not collect, transmit, or store any of your personal data on our servers.** 
All data entered into the application (including party details, financial transactions, and reminders) is stored locally on your device's internal storage using a secure database.

## Biometric Data
The app offers an App Lock feature (fingerprint/face recognition) to protect your data from unauthorized access. This authentication is handled entirely by your Android device's operating system. The app receives only a success/failure callback and does not collect, store, or have direct access to your biometric data.

## Backups
You have the option to export backups (both standard and encrypted) of your database to your device's local storage. This process is entirely controlled by you. We do not automatically upload backups to any cloud service, external server, or third party.

## Third-Party Services
The app does not integrate with any third-party trackers, analytics, or advertising networks that collect personal data.

## Device Permissions
The app requests certain permissions to function correctly:
- **Notifications & Alarms**: Used strictly to send you timely reminders for scheduled tasks and payment follow-ups.
- **Biometrics**: Used strictly to unlock the app if you enable the security feature.

## Changes to This Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this policy.
